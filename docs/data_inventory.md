306 subjects are in DM, of whom **254 were randomized** and 52 were screen failures. The screen failures have no questionnaire, laboratory or medical-history records at all.

This drives every eligibility rule: a criterion is evaluated against the randomized population, because a screen failure was *correctly* excluded and flagging one would be a false positive. Subjects with no data for a criterion are reported as **not evaluable** rather than as passing.

## Reading notes

**Denormal zeros.** The XPORT files decode a stored zero as `5.397605e-79` rather than `0.0`. Sums are unaffected, but comparisons and null tests are not: a questionnaire item scored zero would otherwise compare as greater than zero. `p2d.ingest.reader` snaps these to exact zero on read, and a regression test guards it.

**Character padding.** Character columns arrive space-padded to fixed width, which breaks equality against controlled terminology. They are stripped on read.
